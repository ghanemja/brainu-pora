# Drag polar

The **drag polar** is the relationship between lift coefficient Cl and drag coefficient Cd for an aircraft, typically shown as a curve.

## Form

Cd = Cd0 + k·Cl²

- Cd0 = parasite drag coefficient
- k = induced drag factor

## Significance

Defines aircraft performance: max range, endurance, glide ratio. Critical for [[Mach_number]] design tradeoffs.
