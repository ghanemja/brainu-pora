# Synaptic plasticity

**Synaptic plasticity** is the activity-dependent modification of synaptic strength, the cellular basis of learning and memory.

## Forms

- Long-term potentiation (LTP)
- Long-term depression (LTD)
- Spike-timing dependent plasticity (STDP)

## Hebbian rule

"Cells that fire together, wire together." Connects to [[Action_potential]] timing and [[Ion_channel]] dynamics.
