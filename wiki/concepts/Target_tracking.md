# Target tracking

**Target tracking** is the process of estimating target position, velocity, and identity over time from noisy sensor data. Foundation of radar systems, missile guidance, and surveillance.

## Methods

- Nearest-neighbor data association
- Joint probabilistic data association (JPDA)
- Multiple hypothesis tracking (MHT)
- Random finite set (RFS) filters

## Defense Applications

- Air defense systems
- Anti-ballistic missile defense
- Maritime surveillance
