# Navier-Stokes equations

The **Navier–Stokes equations** are nonlinear PDEs governing momentum balance in viscous fluids. The Clay Millennium Prize problem is to prove existence and smoothness in 3D.

## Equation (incompressible)

ρ(∂v/∂t + v·∇v) = -∇p + μ∇²v + f

## Significance

Foundation of computational fluid dynamics (CFD). Governs everything from aircraft design to weather prediction.
