# Reynolds number

The **Reynolds number** Re is a dimensionless quantity that characterizes the ratio of inertial forces to viscous forces in fluid flow. It predicts whether flow will be laminar or turbulent.

## Formula

Re = ρuL / μ

Where:
- ρ = fluid density
- u = flow velocity
- L = characteristic length
- μ = dynamic viscosity

## Regimes

- Re < 2300: laminar flow
- 2300 < Re < 4000: transitional
- Re > 4000: turbulent
