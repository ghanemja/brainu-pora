# Action potential

An **action potential** is a rapid voltage spike across a neuron's membrane, the fundamental signal of nervous systems.

## Phases

1. Resting (-70 mV)
2. Depolarization (sodium influx)
3. Repolarization (potassium efflux)
4. Hyperpolarization
5. Return to rest

## Hodgkin-Huxley model

Coupled ODEs describe gating variables. Connects to [[State_space_model]] and [[Lyapunov_stability]].
