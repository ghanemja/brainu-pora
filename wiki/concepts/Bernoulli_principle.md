# Bernoulli principle

**Bernoulli's principle** states that an increase in fluid speed corresponds to a decrease in static pressure or potential energy. Foundation of aerodynamic lift.

## Equation

P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant

## Applications

- Aircraft wing lift generation
- Venturi tubes for flow measurement
- Pitot tubes for airspeed sensing
