# Lyapunov stability

**Lyapunov stability** is a property of dynamical systems where trajectories starting near an equilibrium remain bounded.

## Direct method

Find a positive-definite Lyapunov function V(x) where dV/dt ≤ 0 along trajectories. Existence proves stability without solving the ODE.

## Types

- Stable in the sense of Lyapunov
- Asymptotically stable
- Exponentially stable
