# Lift coefficient

**Lift coefficient** Cl is a dimensionless parameter relating lift force to fluid density, velocity, and reference area.

## Formula

Cl = L / (½ρv²A)

## Use

Cl varies with angle of attack until stall. Aircraft cruise around Cl ≈ 0.3–0.5; high-lift devices push to 1.5+.
