# Diffusion

**Diffusion** is the net movement of molecules from regions of high concentration to low concentration, driven by random thermal motion.

## Fick's laws

- 1st: J = -D·∇c
- 2nd: ∂c/∂t = D·∇²c

## Applications

- Cellular transport ([[Membrane_potential]])
- Heat conduction (Fourier's law analog)
- Mass transport in fluids ([[Boundary_layer]])
