# Compressible flow

**Compressible flow** is fluid flow where density changes are significant, typically when [[Mach_number]] exceeds 0.3.

## Phenomena

- Shock waves at supersonic speeds
- Choked flow in nozzles
- Density gradients across boundary layers

## Equations

Augmented [[Navier_Stokes_equations]] with energy and continuity coupled to thermodynamic state.
