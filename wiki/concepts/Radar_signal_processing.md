# Radar signal processing

**Radar signal processing** extracts target detections, tracks, and classifications from raw radar returns. Critical for defense surveillance and air traffic control.

## Pipeline

1. Pulse compression
2. Doppler processing
3. CFAR detection
4. Track formation ([[Target_tracking]])

## Defense Applications

- AESA radars
- Synthetic aperture radar (SAR)
- Ground moving target indicator (GMTI)
