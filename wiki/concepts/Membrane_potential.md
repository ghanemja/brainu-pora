# Membrane potential

The **membrane potential** is the voltage difference across a cell membrane, driven by ionic concentration gradients.

## Nernst equation

E_ion = (RT/zF)·ln([out]/[in])

## Maintained by

- Ion pumps (Na+/K+ ATPase)
- [[Ion_channel]] selective permeability
- [[Diffusion]] gradients
