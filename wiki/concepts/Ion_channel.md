# Ion channel

**Ion channels** are membrane proteins forming pores that selectively conduct ions across cell membranes.

## Types

- Voltage-gated (Na+, K+, Ca2+)
- Ligand-gated (NMDA, GABA-A)
- Mechanically-gated
- Light-gated (channelrhodopsin)

## Significance

Critical for [[Action_potential]] generation, [[Membrane_potential]] regulation, and [[Synaptic_plasticity]].
