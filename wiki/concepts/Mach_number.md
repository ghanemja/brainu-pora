# Mach number

**Mach number** M is the ratio of flow velocity to local speed of sound. Determines compressibility regime.

## Regimes

- M < 0.3: incompressible
- 0.3 < M < 0.8: subsonic
- 0.8 < M < 1.2: transonic
- 1.2 < M < 5: supersonic
- M > 5: hypersonic
