PoRA Atlas

Every concept in the free robot autonomy textbook from Stanford, laid out as the stack it describes: foundations at the bottom, then dynamics and control, planning, perception, estimation, and decisions. Tell it what you already know and what you want to learn, and it orders the chapters, notebooks, and lectures you need.

Unofficial. PoRA Atlas is an independent study aid. It is not made by or affiliated with the book's authors, Stanford University, or Cambridge University Press. Concept summaries and prerequisite links are our own reading of the material; the book is the authoritative reference.

Book: Principles of Robot Autonomy by Gammelli, Lorenzetti, Luo, Zardini, and Pavone. Exercises: StanfordASL/pora-exercises. Lectures: Stanford AA174A and AA203 recordings.
